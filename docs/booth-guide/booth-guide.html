InfoCapture at the booth

Scan a badge or snap a business card. Every contact lands in the team list.

The day before about 5 minutes, on your iPhone

  1. Open the Camera app, point it at the QR code on the right and tap the link that appears. (Or open Safari and go to travis-tlctech.github.io/infocapture.)
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add. From now on, open InfoCapture from this icon, not from Safari.
  3. Open the app and tap the settings icon (top right). Sign in with the email and password Travis gave you.
  4. Under Capturing, fill in Your name and the Current event, then tap Save settings.
  5. Tap Test connection. You should see green ticks for On the team and Card reader is working. Anything in red: tell Travis.

Before doors open

Open the app. Check the event bar (A) shows today's event (tap it to change it) and the top corner (B) says Synced.

Capture a contact three ways

Snap card (C): take a photo of the business card, or of the badge. Start typing notes straight away; the name, company and the rest fill in a few seconds later. Shaded fields came from the card. A field marked check is one the reader wasn't sure of, so compare it with the photo. If the card lists two people, tap Add as a contact for the second one.
Scan QR (D): hold the badge's QR code inside the frame. If no camera picture appears, tap Tap to start the camera. If the app says This badge code has no contact details in it, the code is only an attendee number for the organiser's own app: go back and use Snap card on the badge instead.
Type it in (E): no card and no badge. Type what you have.

Then tap the tags that fit (Hot lead, Follow up, Send info...), add a note about the conversation, and tap Save contact. If the app says Already captured, you have this person already: tap Open and add to that one instead.

The status in the top corner (B)

SyncedEverything is in the team list. Each contact in your list shows a green tick (F). 3 to sendSaved on your phone and waiting for signal ("3 offline" when there is none). They send on their own; keep capturing. Sign inTap it and sign in again. Sync issueTap Retry. If it stays, tell Travis.
While anything is waiting to send, don't sign out and don't delete the app from your Home Screen. Contacts that haven't sent yet live only on your phone.

After the event

  • Tap any contact to fix details or add notes. Changes update the same contact in the team list.
  • Settings > Download spreadsheet gives an Excel file with everyone's contacts. Its card photo links work for 7 days; download again for fresh ones.
  • Optional: Settings > Remove synced contacts from this phone clears your list. Nothing leaves the team list.
